Top 12 activities Tanzania Mwanza

Tanzania Mwanza 

By John Smith posted July 20, 2016

Mwanza, often referred to as "Rock City" due to its unique rock formations, is a vibrant city on the southern shores of Lake Victoria in Tanzania. Here are the top 10 tourist activities to consider when visiting Mwanza:

1 Bismarck Rock

A prominent granite rock formation emerging from Lake Victoria, Bismarck Rock is Mwanza's iconic landmark. It's a popular spot for photography and offers scenic views of the lake.

MORE

2 Saanane Island National Park

As the first national park located within a city in Tanzania, Saanane Island offers wildlife viewing, picnic spots, and walking trails. Visitors can spot animals like zebras, impalas, and various bird species.

MORE

3. Sukuma Museum

Dedicated to the Sukuma culture, Tanzania's largest ethnic group, this museum showcases traditional artifacts, dance performances, and insights into the Sukuma way of life..

MORE

4. Rubondo Island National Park

Located on Lake Victoria, this park is a haven for wildlife enthusiasts, offering sightings of chimpanzees, elephants, and diverse bird species. It's accessible via boat from Mwanza

MORE

5. Boat Tours on Lake Victoria

Experience the vastness of Africa's largest lake through guided boat tours, which offer opportunities for bird watching, fishing, and enjoying picturesque sunsets. 

MORE

6. Mwanza City Tour

Explore the bustling streets, local markets, and cultural sites of Mwanza to get a feel of the city's vibrant life and history. 

MORE

7. Ukerewe Island

As the largest island in Lake Victoria, Ukerewe offers a tranquil retreat with opportunities for cycling, cultural tours, and interacting with local communities.

MORE

8. Rock Paintings

Discover ancient rock paintings in the surrounding areas of Mwanza, which provide insights into the region's early inhabitants and their cultures.
